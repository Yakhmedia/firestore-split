Refund & Returns Policy

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

At Fire Store we want you to be fully satisfied with your purchase. This policy explains how returns, warranty claims, and refunds work for the hardware and accessories we sell.

1. 30-Day Returns

You may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a refund, provided they are unused and in their original packaging with all accessories included.

  • Contact our support team before returning an item so we can issue return instructions.
  • Return shipping for a change-of-mind return is the customer's responsibility; we cover return shipping for faulty or incorrectly shipped items.
  • Refunds are issued to your original payment method once the returned item is received and inspected.

2. 2-Year Warranty

Our hardware is covered by a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects. If a device fails under normal use within the warranty period, contact us and we will repair or replace it, or issue a refund if a replacement is not available.

3. How to Start a Return or Warranty Claim

Message us on WhatsApp or email with your order number and a short description of the issue. Our support team will confirm eligibility and send next steps, usually within a couple of minutes during business hours.

4. Refund Processing Time

Once a refund is approved it is processed immediately. The time for funds to appear depends on your payment method:

  • Credit/Debit Cards (via Stripe): 1 to 3 business days, depending on your bank.
  • PayPal: 1 to 2 days, typically to your PayPal balance or original source.
  • Cryptocurrency: Sent back to your wallet address within 24 hours of approval.

5. Items That Cannot Be Returned

For hygiene and safety reasons, items that have been physically damaged through misuse, or items missing their original packaging and accessories, may not be eligible for a full refund. We will always tell you up front if an item does not qualify.
