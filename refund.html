Subscription Refund Policy

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

You may request a full refund within 7 days of payment. This policy applies to standard Fire Store TV renewal plans and the returning customer welcome-back offer.

1. Request a Refund Within 7 Days

Email contact@firestore.tv from the address used at checkout within 7 days of payment. Include your order number. We will refund the full amount paid to the original payment method. Your bank or card provider determines how long the refund takes to appear; this is typically 5 to 10 business days.

2. Requests After 7 Days

After the 7-day period, we do not normally refund a purchase. We will review a genuine fault on our side. Contact support with your order number and details of the issue.

3. How to Reach Us

For subscription refund questions, email us from the address used at checkout. You can also contact support by WhatsApp:
