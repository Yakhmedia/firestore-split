---

## 1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Update the current Fire Store homepage from a World Cup-focused landing page into a current football-season landing page.

The new homepage should:

1. Remove outdated World Cup messaging.
2. Introduce a strong "Football Is Back" seasonal hook.
3. Improve message match between email campaigns and landing page.
4. Increase renewals from expired customers.
5. Increase trial requests from undecided customers.
6. Improve subscription-plan positioning.
7. Improve credibility and trust.
8. Reduce unsupported or overly aggressive claims.
9. Improve mobile conversion.
10. Maintain broader entertainment positioning beyond football.

## 16. PRICING VALIDATION

Use:

```text
Monthly Baseline × Number of Months = Regular Cost
Regular Cost - Plan Price = Dollar Savings
```

Examples:

```text
$19 × 6 = $114
$114 - $89 = $25 savings
```

```text
$19 × 12 = $228
$228 - $149 = $79 savings
```

Do not use incorrect percentage discounts.

#### Objective

Promote the Fire Store streaming app as a major product advantage and a simpler alternative to using third-party player apps.

The app should solve a clear customer problem:

```text
OLD EXPERIENCE
Download a third-party player

→ Enter a separate username and password
→ Configure a long server address
→ Repeat setup on additional devices
```

```text
FIRE STORE APP EXPERIENCE
Download the Fire Store app
→ Sign in with email and password
→ Start watching
```

The app should be positioned as part of the Fire Store experience, not merely as another IPTV player.
