Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

At Fire Store (fire-store.tv), accessible from https://fire-store.tv, the privacy of our visitors and customers is a priority. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide when placing an order or contacting support. This includes:

  • Personal & order information: Name, email address, WhatsApp phone number, shipping address, and the details of the products you order.
  • Payment details: All payments are handled securely by external payment processors (Stripe, PayPal, or crypto processors). We do not store your complete payment card details on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in order to:

  • Process, fulfil, and ship your orders.
  • Provide setup support and send order and shipping updates via WhatsApp or email.
  • Handle returns, warranty claims, and customer support requests.
  • Improve, personalise, and analyse our website and its performance.
  • Prevent fraud and keep our store secure.

3. Log Files & Analytics

Fire Store follows a standard procedure of using log files. These log visitors when they use websites and may include IP address, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, and referring/exit pages. This information is not linked to anything personally identifiable. We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic and usage patterns and improve the site.

4. Advertising & Conversion Tracking

We use Google Ads conversion tracking to measure the effectiveness of our advertising for our products. These tags collect anonymous, aggregate data about how visitors reach and interact with our store. You can control ad personalisation through your Google account's ad settings.

5. Third-Party Privacy Policies

This Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We advise you to consult the respective privacy policies of our third-party service providers (such as Stripe, PayPal, and Google) for more detailed information.

6. GDPR & CCPA Data Protection Rights

We want you to be fully aware of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following:

  • The right to access – request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification – request that we correct information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure – request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict/object to processing – request that we restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.

7. Security

We employ standard security measures (SSL encryption, secure APIs) to protect your name, email, address, and WhatsApp number. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
